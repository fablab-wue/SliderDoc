Legend: DRV_ENABLE GP0; motor1 STEP/DIR/LIMIT GP1–4; motor2 GP5–8.
ERROR 1–3 GP9–11; UART 115200 baud GP12/13; motor3 STEP/DIR GP27/26, LIMIT3 GP15/14.
SERVO_1..3 GP23/22/21 (underside). CAMERA_CTRL GP29 (OC / T). LED GP25; GP16 onboard WS2812.
EXT_1..2 GP17/18; motor4 DIR/STEP GP19/20 takes EXT_3/EXT_4. CS motors 2|3|4 / CS servos (no RB).
RP2350-Zero (env rp2350zero) reuses this map. SW_LIMIT_*_N off until CS …_use=1. Pin names match IG / VG.
